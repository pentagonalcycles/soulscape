- **Identity**: The anonymous/alias/real identity system (point 3) needs careful design. How do you prevent abuse while maintaining the safe space?

The vision is genuinely inspiring. The real challenge will be scoping it down to something you can actually build and launch, then growing from there. What's your current thinking on priorities?
